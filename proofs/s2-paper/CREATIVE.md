# Last Cue — S2 Paper K-pop proof

**Format:** 16:9 · 15 s · 30 fps
**Shots:** 3 · 4.0 / 5.0 / 6.0 s — deliberately not equal
**Style:** Paper K-pop 0.1.0
**Director's note:** this is the creative direction for the proof. In the future
this page, the brief and the resolved format are inputs *before* any rendering.

## Logline

Nova Kim's last entrance of the night: a red cue lamp in the wings, a crowd that
comes through the wall as a printed line, two paper performers half a beat
apart, and a sticker that calls the house lights.

## Visual direction

- **World:** a cut-paper pop stage on warm cream card, hard top-left key, no
  gloss, no 3D, no photograph.
- **Palette (day-stage):** paper, ink, red, acid; gold accent used twice.
- **Type:** Anton display, Archivo Black body, DM Mono labels, Permanent Marker
  monogram. Cream cut edge, hard ink shadow, 12 Hz boil.
- **Cast:** Nova Kim and one paper backup, Mira. Two performers maximum.
- **Motion language:** paper snap, sticker slam with squash/overshoot, stacked
  type drop, stage build-on, eight-count dance, confetti, halftone and fibre boil.
- **Sound (future):** 88 BPM electronic pop, paper foley; this proof is silent.

## What is moving in every shot

- Paper layers drift by 2–6 px with the camera; no static flat plate.
- Type drops and settles; the 12 Hz boil makes the edge breathe.
- Halftone, fibre, key light, confetti and shards are alive.
- Two performers bounce, sway, point and settle on the beat; the backup is a
  different character, not a mirror.
- The camera pushes, orbits or pulses through the shot, not only at the cut.
- The transition is part of the shot: iris, peel or wipe, never a slideshow fade.

## Constraints

- No source film story, lyric, character name or scene.
- Four colour roles per frame; gold accent twice.
- Eight words per content slot.
- Two performers maximum.
- No full film; this is a proof excerpt.

## Future input shape

A real film should arrive as:

1. a content brief,
2. a resolved format with shot roles and durations,
3. a detailed treatment / beat sheet / shot sheet like this one,
4. optional per-shot motion notes and sound cues.

The engine and style backend consume those; they do not invent the creative
direction.
