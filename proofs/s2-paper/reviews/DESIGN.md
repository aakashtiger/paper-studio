# Designer / DOP review

## Pass 1 — 5 / 10

Competent flat vector with paper texture, not premium paper-craft. Layers scaled
together, so the push read as a zoom on a plate; the title was cramped; the mono
line was cream on orange; the stage was two mirrored figures on a flat floor.

Fixes applied: per-layer parallax, title edge and tracking, mono on a cream tab,
larger sticker, print strip, ghost 07, date ticket, peel, flat blue floor with
cream rules, three crowd tiers, stronger key light, a second character.

## Pass 2 — 7 / 10

Shot 2 is the most premium frame: torn red field, hard tab shadow, halftone,
date ticket. Shot 1 reads designed. Shot 3 still reads as flat cutouts stacked
on a plate.

**Still open:**

1. Contact shadows: the cast hovers; the boots end in air. Add an ink ellipse
   ~120 × 18 px at each sole and a smaller one at each ankle.
2. The character rig is the weak point: arms read as detached cream tubes at
   this size, hands are absent, legs taper into boots. Either shorten the
   figures or rebuild the arm (92 px upper / 78 px forearm, 26 px wide, 3 px ink
   outline, 2 px cream edge, mitten hands).
3. Background depth: the sun casts no shadow on the rays, so the stage
   collapses into one plate. A hard duplicate at +16/+18, ink 22%, no blur.
4. Palette discipline: the blue peel backing, blue skirt, blue floor and blue
   confetti make six roles in one frame. Four is the rule.

**Removed or changed after this review:** the vertical mono strip (collided
with the peel and clipped), the ring reduced to the lower arc, the peel backing
deepened to `blueD`, the ghost 07 moved fully on screen.

**Verdict:** the look can carry the style. The rig and the dance are the next
quality ceiling, not the palette.
