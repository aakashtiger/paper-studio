# Motion director review — first pass

**Score:** 4.5 / 10 — not yet “not a slideshow” at the reference bar.

## Strengths

- The sticker frame is the best in the piece: torn red bed, halftone, burst,
  tape, barcode and Anton on cream.
- The type system and print texture hold up at 1080p; the palette is disciplined.
- 4 / 5 / 6 s shot lengths are deliberately unequal and the cut into the stage is
  confident.

## Blocking issues

1. The sticker shot was a single static plate for most of its 5 s; the stage
   repeated one small sine bounce. The camera push was 3–5% across a whole shot.
2. The open iris dipped to black; the stage exit held electric blue. Both broke
   the paper world.
3. The stage had mirrored identical performers, fixed arms, no depth and a frozen
   meter/lower third.

## Fixes applied after this review

- Sticker: slow rotation/scale, a punch-in on the beat, a second shard burst and a
  moving light read.
- Stage: four-beat pose phrases (point, turn, hair whip), 18 px bounce, head turn,
  hair swing, beat-pulsed camera, ticking meter and a lower third that lands late.
- Open: paper shard burst before the iris.
- Irises: red at the open, cream/paper at the close.

## Still needed

- A second motion review on the rendered MP4, not the stills.
- More secondary motion on the crowd and set; sound is still silent.
