## Questions

- **`paper-shadow` — Collage or Print?** The pack leaves it open. Collage adds contact shadows and diorama depth (most visibly in the shot 6 pull-back); Print keeps the layers flat with only the type shadow. Does a Collage contact shadow count against the four-colour budget, and which option does the director want?
- **Frame rate and the 12 Hz boil:** the "12 Hz, held on twos" rule is exact at 24 fps, not 30 fps; the pack states neither the delivery frame rate nor the cadence S2 should use. Which applies?
- **Type shadow offset and cream lip:** the pack fixes the direction and the edge but gives no pixel offset or lip thickness at 390 px or 9:16. What are the numbers?
- **9:16 cover vs re-stage:** the aspect rules say images fill with cover, while the adaptation says re-stage both performers and never crop. For `stage_wide` and `pullback_close`, does the renderer re-pose the paper rig and diorama, or cover-crop them?
- **Beat offset:** does the 0.21 s offset move the first cut, and is the 30.000 s target measured from frame 0 or the first downbeat? Which end absorbs quantisation?
- **Two irises:** is a second iris in a six-shot film allowed, or should one become a cut without editing the pack?
- **Audio and voice:** what original 30 s track is cleared, is there a voice line, and is the close's room tone produced, recorded or silent?
- **Loudness:** is `-14 LUFS` the intended delivery target, or should the format carry another?
- **Venue slot:** `content.venue` is unread; is `subtitle` the only home for "The Fold, rooftop", or should the brief also carry the venue for metadata?
- **`frame` fill:** is `type_stack`'s red `frame` a filled plate or an outline? If an outline, paper type on cream disappears.
