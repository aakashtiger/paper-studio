## Grammar check

- **Cut-paper edge:** Shot 1 — the cream card and the open sun carry the lighter cream lip and the dark ink line; the same edge returns in Shot 3's paper window and Shot 6's diorama cutaway. The edge stays visible under either `paper-shadow` option.
- **Cream type edge and hard ink shadow:** Shot 2 — "Night Fold" and "Bring a coat" are paper letters with a cream cut edge and one fixed misregistered ink shadow; the same cream edge and ink-shadow treatment lands on Shot 3's "Vera Lune" and Shot 5's sticker.
- **Torn fibre or sticker:** Shot 5 — the red torn bed and the paper sticker land flat with a squash and no bounce; Shot 1's paper fibre sits at eighteen percent.
- **Halftone:** Shot 2 at 0.22 and Shot 4 at 0.18 — a fixed dot screen with one angle, never an airbrush.
- **Two-cast rig:** Shot 4 — `stage_wide` uses the lead and the second as layered paper puppets on visible eyelets, in separate lead and second boxes; there is no third figure.
- **Beat cut or beat-locked move:** Shot 4 — the hard cut out of the orbit into Shot 5 is planned on the 88 BPM grid; Shot 5's sticker slam lands flat on the beat. S2 makes both frame-exact.
- **9:16 re-stage:** Shot 4 — stage, lead, second, body, caption and meter re-anchor to the 9:16 `stage`, `lead`, `second`, `bodyLower`, `caption` and `meter` boxes; the 16:9 stage is not cropped. Shot 1's sun/title and Shot 5's sticker/text re-anchor the same way.

## Questions

- **`paper-shadow` (open choice):** Collage adds a soft contact shadow to every paper layer; Print keeps the layers flat with only the fixed type shadow. Both keep the visible cut edge. Shot 4's two-cast stage and Shot 6's diorama read deeper under Collage and flatter under Print; the director must choose: Collage or Print?
- **Second performer:** `stage_wide` needs `character.second`, but the brief has one `names` line and the portrait is one lead. Is the second an uncredited foil, or does the second need a name/credit, and where does it go?
- **Lyric band:** The portrait puts `content.caption` in a strip named the lyric band. May it carry event copy ("Doors 8:30 · 31 Oct"), or does the band want an original vocal line?
- **88 BPM map:** 30 s is 44 beats at 88 BPM before the 0.21 s offset. The pack gives plan milliseconds, not a six-shot beat map; which cuts are frame-exact, and how are the 44 beats shared across shots that plan at 4.855–5.193 s?
- **Loudness:** The pack gives mix character but no delivery target. The format uses -14 LUFS; confirm.
- **Palette:** This treatment uses the default day-stage; confirm, or switch to night-stage?
- **Pack defect — corner marks:** `pullback_close` places the 16:9 corner marks at a 3% inset, outside the 6% top/side safe margin; its 9:16 map uses the 6% `screen` box. Which is correct for the 16:9 stage? This film's 9:16 close uses the 6% map.
