# Questions — Night Fold

- **`paper-shadow` (open choice):** Collage gives every paper layer a soft contact shadow and keeps the visible cut edge; Print keeps the layers flat with the same visible cut edge and gives only the type its fixed hard offset shadow. In this route the two-cast stage (Shot 4) and the diorama close (Shot 6) gain depth under Collage and read as a flat printed pop-up under Print; the cut edge and type shadow are unchanged. The director must choose: Collage or Print? The treatment is staged to work under either.
- **Second performer:** `stage_wide` requires `character.second`, but the brief has one `names` value and `portrait_iris` is a one-lead portrait. Is the second an uncredited foil, or should the second be named and credited? If named, which slot carries it without turning the portrait into the two-cast portrait the pack says is a future recipe?
- **Lyric band or event caption:** `portrait_iris` places `content.caption` in a strip named the lyric band. Is a non-lyric event caption ("Doors 8:30 · 31 Oct") acceptable there, or does the band want an original vocal line? No third-party lyric is used either way.
- **88 BPM quantisation:** 30 s at 88 BPM is 44 beats before the 0.21 s offset. The resolver returns shot lengths in milliseconds and the pack defers frame-exact cuts to S2, but gives no six-shot beat map. Which cuts are frame-exact, and how are the 44 beats shared across six shots that plan at 4.855–5.193 s?
- **Delivery loudness:** The pack gives a mix character but no delivery target. The format uses -14 LUFS; is that the intended target?
- **Palette confirmation:** The pack leaves day-stage/night-stage to the director and defaults to day-stage. This treatment names day-stage; confirm, or switch to night-stage?
- **Pack defect — corner-mark safe margin:** `pullback_close` places the 16:9 `hud.corner-marks` at x/y 0.03 with w/h 0.94 (a 3% inset), outside the pack's 6% top/side safe margin; the 9:16 vertical map uses the 6% `screen` box.
