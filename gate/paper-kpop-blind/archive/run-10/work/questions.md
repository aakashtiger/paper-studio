# Questions — FOLDLIGHT

1. **`paper-shadow` (open choice).** Collage would give every cut layer a soft contact shadow and make the rooftop read as a built diorama; Print would keep the same visible cut edges but only the type would cast, so the film reads as a printed poster. The choice changes the depth of shots 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6, and whether the HUD (lyric band, meter sticker, corner marks) also casts. The hard type shadow is fixed either way. The director has not answered, and this treatment does not choose.
2. **Palette carriage.** The treatment names night-stage, but `day-stage`/`night-stage` is a director choice and not a resolver field. Is a line in the treatment enough, or must the brief or format carry it? If not, how does the resolver know which paper stock to use?
3. **Sequence validity and `list`.** The pack closes the eight roles but gives no order, repetition or completeness rule. Is `open → title → portrait → list → quote → close` valid, and may `list` be a two-cast stage? `stage_wide` declares `list` but has no list slot; its `content.body` and `content.caption` are single values.
4. **Recipe literals vs treatment tinting.** `STYLE.md` says the treatment assigns the four palette roles per shot and assets are role-tinted, but recipes hardcode colours (for example `paper_open` has an acid sunburst and accent core; `stage_wide` has an acid burst). Which wins if a treatment wants a different plate?
5. **Colour budget.** Is the meter's acid readout a frame colour, and is its black sticker `ink`? Does a role count once or once per asset? Is the accent's “at least twice” counted by shot or by visible swatch, and is a route without `paper_open` or `sticker_slam` invalid?
6. **Transition seam.** Does a shot play its own `transitionOut`, the next recipe's entrance `effect`, or both? `portrait_iris` carries both an `effect` of kind `iris` and `transitionOut: "iris"`; `paper_open` also exits on an iris. The precedence decision covers camera and transition ops, not effect-vs-transition.
7. **Beat quantisation.** The resolver's seconds are plan values and the 88 BPM grid is S2. Are the returned seconds pre- or post-quantisation? Where does the 0.21 s offset land, and is rounding cumulative across six cuts?
8. **Word counting.** What counts as a word for the eight-word limit: punctuation-attached tokens, numerals, slashes, hyphenated names, the monogram? Is `THE FOLD, ROOFTOP` three words or four?
9. **Slot reuse.** One `content.caption` must serve the portrait's lyric band and the stage lower-third; one `content.body` is body, lower-third, display and close line across four recipes. Must each line be short enough for the tightest box (the sticker's 0.6 × 0.16 display box), or may auto-fit shrink it below the display scale while holding the 14 px floor?
10. **Meter readout.** “Beat count and four-role bar … generated from the plan”: is the count per shot or global, and does the bar show the roles in that frame or the film's palette?
11. **Loudness.** The format carries `-14 LUFS`. The pack says delivery loudness belongs to the format but gives no integrated target, true-peak ceiling or measurement method. Is -14 LUFS the intended value, and is the metadata advisory only?
12. **9:16 orbit.** `stage_wide` keeps the 16:9 orbit 1→1.06 on the 9:16 re-stage. Should the orbit centre on the two-cast pair in the central 60% or on the stage box, and does the orbit preserve the “no handheld” rule at that scale?
13. **Card/SOURCES mismatch (pack defect).** `SOURCES.md` says the card uses portrait, chorus-stage and macro-prop; `card.html` actually shows portrait-paper-cast (00:03), throne-orbit (00:14) and open-sunflower (00:00). Which set is the evidence of record? The card's own aria-label agrees with its images, not with `SOURCES.md`.
14. **Date/venue type mapping (pack inconsistency).** `STYLE.md` says date, venue and meter use label, but `capabilities.slots` says date and venue are not read and no recipe has those slots. Is the mapping stale, or is there an unlisted date/venue slot?
