- **Cut-paper edge:** Shot 1 (`paper_open`) — the sun's petals, its core and the title's cream lip sit on the card with a visible cut edge. Shot 6 (`pullback_close`) — the diorama's cutaway edge and the small paper object's cut edge.

- **Torn fibre or sticker:** Shot 3 (`portrait_iris`) — the torn ink lyric band under the caption. Shot 5 (`sticker_slam`) — the red torn bed and the paper sticker, with visible fibre.
- **Halftone:** Shot 4 (`stage_wide`) — the fixed-angle dot screen over the stage; it also prints under the type in shots 2 and 5.
- **Two-cast rig:** Shot 4 (`stage_wide`) — Vera (lead) and the unnamed double (second) are the only two figures; both are layered paper puppets with visible eyelet joints. There is no third figure.
- **Beat cut or beat-locked move:** Shot 4 (`stage_wide`) — the orbit holds the stage flat and lands its move on the 88 BPM bar; the cut into shot 5 lands on the next beat. Shot 5's sticker slam lands flat on the beat.
- **9:16 re-stage:** Shot 4 (`stage_wide`) — the two figures re-stage into the central 60%, the body drops to the lower third and the meter re-anchors top right. Shot 3 (`portrait_iris`) — the portrait, window, names and lyric band re-anchor to the 9:16 boxes. Shot 1's title re-breaks to two lines for the 9:16 title box. This is a re-stage, not a crop.

## Questions

The full audit is in `questions.md`. The questions that change this treatment are:

- **`paper-shadow`:** Collage or Print? The choice changes the sun and core in shot 1, the window and lyric band in shot 3, the stage and cast in shot 4, and most of all the final diorama pull-back in shot 6. The treatment is written to work under either; the choice is not answered here.
- **Palette:** day-stage is proposed. If night-stage is wanted, the accent turns pink and the rooftop becomes a night stage; the route still fits. Confirm.
- **Second cast and colour:** is the second an unnamed double? If Vera's jacket is a red plate and the second keeps the accent sleeve, shot 4 would carry paper, ink, red, acid and accent (five roles). The budget says drop the accent first. Which plate drops?
- **Music and voice:** which cleared 88 BPM track, and is there a voice line? The pack supplies no audio.
- **Copy:** `content.venue` and `content.date` are unread; the venue is in `subtitle` and the date is in the brief only. Is that enough?
- **Type:** the 9:16 title breaks as “LAST LIGHT / CHECK” and the sticker as “DOORS AT / EIGHT”. Confirm those breaks. What string should the meter read?
- **Beat grid:** where does the 0.21 s offset land, and which shot absorbs the 30 s rounding?
- **Sequence grammar:** is open → title → portrait → list → quote → close valid, or is there a preferred order? The route omits `detail` and `transition`.
- **Pack defects:** the lyric band position and width, the corner-mark margins, the `sticker_slam` and `type_stack` shadow targets, the `pullback_close`/`type_stack` rule order, and the card's Print red type shadow.
