## Grammar check

- **Cut-paper edge:** Shot 1 (`paper_open`) — the sunburst petals, core burst and title are separate flat cards; each carries the cream lip and dark ink undercut, and the burst sits on the sun rather than blending into it.
- **Cream type edge and hard ink shadow:** Shot 2 (`type_stack`) — the display title and body are paper letters with the cream cut lip and one fixed down-right ink shadow; the acid rule stays a flat printed rule, not a bevel.
- **Torn fibre or sticker:** Shot 5 (`sticker_slam`) — the red bed is a torn strip with visible fibre, and the paper sticker lands flat with a squash; shot 3's lyric band is a second torn strip.
- **Halftone:** Shot 2 (`type_stack`) — the fixed dot screen at 0.22 over the ink card; shot 4 carries the stage's 0.18 screen at one angle, never an airbrush.
- **Two-cast rig:** Shot 4 (`stage_wide`) — Vera and the unnamed second player are two separate layered puppets with visible eyelets and separate arm and leg chains; the second is not a duplicated lead plate.
- **Beat cut or beat-locked move:** Shot 4 to 5 — a hard cut planned on the 88 BPM grid; shot 5's sticker contacts on the next beat after one frame of anticipation and a 0.12 squash.
- **9:16 re-stage:** Shot 4 (`stage_wide`) — the 16:9 formation is re-blocked to the 9:16 anchors: lead at x 0.20, w 0.22; second at x 0.58, w 0.22; stage at x 0.04, y 0.20, w 0.92, h 0.54; meter top right; bodyLower and caption stacked. Both performers are re-staged; the frame is not a centre crop.

## Questions

- `paper-shadow`: Collage or Print? Collage gives every cut layer a soft contact shadow and a visible cut edge; Print keeps the plates flat and leaves only the type's hard offset. Which is this film? Do not answer on the director's behalf.
- If Collage, does the soft contact shadow also sit under the type and the lyric band, or only under the paper pieces? The card's Collage panel shows a cream outline plus a soft type shadow, while Print shows the fixed hard offset; which type treatment is canonical?
- If Print, is the cream cut lip alone enough to read the edge at 390 px in shot 4's wide stage and shot 6's diorama, with no contact shadow?
- The generic `effect kind: "shadow"` in `type_stack` and `sticker_slam` is not marked type-only, and it sits above the type in z-order. Is it the fixed type shadow, or a layer contact shadow that would pre-decide `paper-shadow`?
- The pack defaults to day-stage; this treatment names night-stage. Confirm night-stage, or should the rooftop be day-stage?
- The reference frames exceed the contract: `macro-prop` has more than four colour plates, and `chorus-stage` shows four performers. Are they evidence of material and formation only, with the four-colour and two-cast rules still binding?
- The brief has one `names` slot, but the two-cast shot needs a second performer. Can the second stay unnamed?
- The pack does not read `content.venue` and has no rooftop set. Is the subtitle plus the stage, open sun and diorama cutaway enough to carry "The Fold, rooftop"?
- The lyric band reads `content.caption`, not a lyric. Is an original non-lyric line allowed in the band, or must the band carry a cleared lyric?
- `pullback_close` uses `prop.object`, but the brief has no prop slot. May the director choose the small object (a paper cup) without a brief field?
- The resolver shares the remainder by capacity in whole milliseconds but does not say how the final remainder is rounded or which shot gets the extra millisecond. Is the resolver authoritative over the plan split?
- S2 quantises cuts to the 88 BPM grid with a 0.21 s offset, but the pack gives no tolerance for the 1.2 s minimum or the 5.5 s maximum after quantisation. Which bound wins if a cut lands outside the range?
- `aspectRules.9:16` gives a 0.04 inset for the sun core and 0.06 for the sticker on its torn bed, but the 16:9 recipes place the core at roughly 0.23–0.39 and the sticker at roughly 0.09–0.23 relative to their parents. Which values govern the 9:16 re-stage?
- At 9:16, may display type shrink below 56 px to auto-fit a long line, and what is the floor? The caption and monogram boxes overlap; which moves if a shot uses both?
- The meter is a black sticker with an acid readout, but the pack gives no text format and the card's phone shows an acid "88" sticker instead. Which is canonical, and what does the meter read?
- No audio is supplied. Who clears the track, confirms it is 88 BPM, and owns the optional voice line? There is no clearance field in the brief.
