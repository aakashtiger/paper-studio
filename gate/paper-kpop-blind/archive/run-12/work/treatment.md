# Treatment — Last Soundcheck

Night-stage, 88 BPM. Vera Lune takes the rooftop of The Fold for a last soundcheck before the moonrise show; the paper stage assembles around her, a mirror-cut double joins for one formation, and the diorama pulls back to the roof edge and a paper city. Sound is electronic pop with paper snap/tear foley and one beat of silence before the sticker slam; one short vocal chop, no lyric wall; the cleared track is supplied per film.

## Grammar check

- **Cut-paper edge:** Shot 1, `paper_open` — the acid sun rays and the accent core carry the lighter cream lip and the dark ink undercut; the 18% fibre sits on the cut. The edge is visible under either paper-shadow option.
- **Cream type edge and hard ink shadow:** Shot 2, `type_stack` — "Last / Soundcheck" in paper fill on the red title card has a cream cut lip and one hard ink shadow offset down-right; "Live at moonrise" repeats it at body scale. At 9:16 the sizes are display 56 and body 24.
- **Torn fibre or sticker:** Shot 5, `sticker_slam` — the red torn-strip bed shows the torn fibre edge; the paper sticker carries its peel corner and cut edge. Shot 4's red lyric band is a second torn strip.
- **Halftone:** Shot 3, `stage_wide` — the fixed-angle halftone at 18% prints over the stage and behind both figures, never over the type or as a soft airbrush.
- **Two-cast rig:** Shot 3, `stage_wide` — Vera is the lead paper puppet at x .20; an uncredited mirror-cut double is the second at x .58; both show eyelet joints and a cream cut edge, and no third figure is on stage.
- **Beat cut or beat-locked move:** Shot 2 → shot 3 — the two-line type drop stacks bottom line up on the 88 BPM beat and cuts hard into the stage; shot 5's sticker slam lands flat on the beat and cuts to the close. S2 quantises the cuts to the grid.
- **9:16 re-stage:** Shot 3, `stage_wide` — the vertical map moves the lead to x .20–.42 and the second to x .58–.80 inside the central 60%, places the stage at .20–.74 and re-anchors the meter to the top-right box; it is a re-stage, not a crop.

## Questions

Full list: `questions.md`. The questions that change this treatment are:

- **`paper-shadow`:** Collage or Print? The film is designed to hold either; the choice changes the contact shadows in shots 1–6 and whether the sticker and diorama lift, but not the cut edge or the fixed type shadow. I have not chosen. See `questions.md` for the shot-by-shot effect.
- **`portrait_iris` box order:** The caption and lyric band share the `lyric` box, but the band is listed after the caption; under the pack's nesting rule the band would nest inside the smaller caption, against z-order. Should the band be the parent? If yes, the two vertical entries need swapping; I cannot edit the sealed pack.
- **`sticker_slam` sticker fill:** The body is fixed paper; I assigned the sticker ink so "Live at moonrise" reads. Is that the intended fill, or should the sticker be red or accent?
- **`caption` and `body` overload:** One `caption` serves the lyric band, stage lower-third and macro caption; one `body` serves the title card, lower-third, sticker quote and close. "The Fold, rooftop" and "Live at moonrise" carry all of them; is that the intended split?
- **Beat quantisation:** The plan values are not 88 BPM multiples; which 44-beat map should S2 use (nearest 7/7/8/7/7/8), and does the 0.21 s offset shift the first cut to 0.21 s?
- **Production gaps:** 9:16 raster/fps, type-shadow offset, seed and loudness are not fixed by the pack; the full list is in `questions.md`.
