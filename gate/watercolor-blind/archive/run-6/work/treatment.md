# Treatment — What the Room Kept

## Route

**Subject.** A 30-second song/memory film for Nora Vale (fictional), told through three objects in one side room: a pressed violet, an iron window latch and a chipped clay cup. The room is the subject; the objects are the argument. No on-camera cast, no photograph, no third-party story.

| 2 | `watercolor_specimen` | `detail` | 5.833 | Flying white; Painting-in; Annotation leader; No vector outline |

**Fit and allocation.** Sum of minima = 4 + 5 + 5 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 26 s. Sum of maxima = 8 + 10 + 10 + 8 + 7 + 7 = 50 s. The target 30 s sits inside that fit. The 4 s remainder is shared by remaining capacity (4 + 5 + 5 + 4 + 3 + 3 = 24 s): +0.667, +0.833, +0.833, +0.667, +0.500, +0.500. The six shots are 4.667 / 5.833 / 5.833 / 4.667 / 4.500 / 4.500 s, summing to 30.000 s. This is the stated allocation rule, not an equal split. In whole milliseconds the shares are 667 / 833 / 833 / 667 / 500 / 500 ms; they sum exactly, so no tie-break is needed.

**Shot subjects.**
1. The arched window of the side room, the low band of garden light beyond the sill, the vermilion stamp on the wall, then the title, subtitle, name, room label and date.
2. The pressed violet on the sill: stem and leaves first, then the wide petals with flying white; one hand note on a leader.
3. The iron window latch: a thin form, one clean stroke, the plate and the screw; one hand note on a leader.
4. The chipped clay cup in the four o'clock light: body, rim, chip, handle; the readable line on the left, the note below with a leader.
5. The paper-coloured mist: the room, the cup and the line wash back to the sheet; no caption, no accent.
6. The cup's handle, enlarged: the last wash pass stops at the join, leaving the sheet; the stamp, monogram, closing line and date; a hold.

**One value per slot.** The caption is the same on both specimen plates, the quote note and the close: “She kept small things.” It is a running note and the close's refrain, not a different note per object. The venue is the same on the title and both specimens: “THE SIDE ROOM, 4:00.” The date is the same on the title and close: “4 MAY.” The two specimen entries are two occurrences of `detail`, each with its own allocation and the same recipe values; the objects differ only in what the treatment names, because the asset is a type, not a subject. Per-shot notes are S2 and are not claimed.

**9:16 composition.** The page is 390 × 693 px (9:16); larger frames scale proportionally and the 14 px floor stays. The boxes are `tokens.layout.aspectBoxes.9:16`, not the 1:1 boxes. Title: `title` (0.08, 0.18, 0.5, 0.22), `subtitle` (0.08, 0.42, 0.5, 0.12), `names` (0.08, 0.56, 0.5, 0.06), `arch` (0.58, 0.22, 0.34, 0.4), `seal` (0.84, 0.08, 0.12, 0.0675), `monogram` (0.84, 0.2, 0.12, 0.05), `labelVenue` (0.08, 0.76, 0.6, 0.05), `labelDate` (0.08, 0.82, 0.5, 0.05). Specimens: `specimen` (0.2, 0.2, 0.6, 0.5), `specimenNote` (0.54, 0.3, 0.38, 0.16), `label` (0.08, 0.82, 0.6, 0.05). Quote: `quote` (0.08, 0.24, 0.48, 0.3), `quoteObject` (0.58, 0.16, 0.32, 0.46), `quoteNote` (0.08, 0.6, 0.48, 0.14). Close: `closeDetail` (0.52, 0.16, 0.36, 0.46), `closeWash` (0.48, 0.46, 0.44, 0.28), `closeSeal` (0.1, 0.18, 0.12, 0.0675), `closeMonogram` (0.1, 0.3, 0.2, 0.06), `closeNote` (0.1, 0.38, 0.4, 0.14), `closeDate` (0.1, 0.62, 0.4, 0.06). The title stays in the left column and the window arch on the right. Leader paths are re-anchored: specimen leaders run from the object's upper edge to the left edge of `specimenNote`; the quote leader runs from the cup's lower-left to the right edge of `quoteNote`. The note never crosses the painted form; a 12 px paper halo keeps the Caveat legible over the wash. The close's cup handle is composed so its unfinished join sits at the recipe's `paper-gap` (x 0.62–0.68, y 0.18–0.52); the gap is the sheet, not a white shape.

**Director choices.** Palette `garden-day`: paper #f3ead9, ink #2f2a24, wash #9fb7a4, petal #d9a6a0, accent #c65438. `deep` is the wash at two or three passes, not a darker tube; the cup's shaded side is the same wash at three passes. At most five pigments in a frame: paper, ink and wash throughout, petal on the violet and the quote's ground, the vermilion accent on the two seals. No frame needs more than five. Paint-in speed 1.8 s per painted form: the violet, the latch, the cup, the close's cup handle and the title's window arch. The mist is a wipe, not a paint-in; the seal is a stamp, not a painted form. Camera: title push-in 1.0→1.04 easeOut; both specimens 1.0→1.06 easeOut; quote, mist and close static. No pan; `watercolor_walk` is not used, because its 12 s minimum cannot fit a 30 s six-shot page and a page is a locked sheet. Sound: a wordless solo felt-piano motif, unhurried, no drums; no vocal, no lyric, no third-party song title. Paper-brush rustle at each paint-in; pencil scratch for the specimen notes and the quote note; a soft page turn after each specimen; music out from the mist through the close; paper and room only; one quiet stamp thump at the close. Seed: an integer chosen by the lead; it seeds stroke phase, paper-fibre offset, wash-edge jitter, paint-in order and sway phase; one seed across all six shots. Palette, type family, recipe order and camera direction are fixed, not seeded. No sway: the objects are still; the only motion is the paint-in and the small push-in. The 48 BPM grid is a breath reference, not a beat lock.

**Why this is not the pack's example.** The pack's example is a garden wedding. This film has no couple, ceremony, table or garden arch: the title's arch is the side room's window, the objects are a violet, a latch and a cup, and the story is a room remembered. The line and the note are original prose; the film uses no lyric, no song title, no voice and no photograph.

## Invariants

- **Paper is the white:** Shot 1: the sheet behind the title's horizon wash and window arch is `paper` #f3ead9; no pure-white paint is used. Shot 5: the mist is a paper-coloured wash that erases the quote back to the sheet. Shot 6: the gap in the cup handle is the sheet showing through, not a white shape.
- **Flying white:** Shot 2: the violet's broad petal strokes carry 3–9 bristle tracks that break into dashes; the stem is one clean stroke. Shot 3 is the counter-test: the latch is a thin form and gets one clean stroke, not noise. The bristle count is 3–9 on the wide strokes only.
- **Pooled edge:** Shot 1: the horizon wash and the window arch are three passes of the same shape with jittered vertices; the edge darkens where the passes disagree. Shot 3: the edge-wash behind the latch pools where its three passes disagree. Shot 4: the cup's object-wash pools at the rim. Shot 6: the unfinished wash pools at the paper gap and stops.
- **Painting-in:** Shot 2: the violet paints in from first stroke to last over 1.8 s, structure before detail (stem, leaves, petals, then the note). Shot 4: the cup paints in by the same 1.8 s rule. Shot 6: the cup handle paints in and stops before the join; the last pass leaves the gap. Shot 1's window arch also paints in at 1.8 s; the mist is a wipe, not a paint-in.
- **Annotation leader:** Shots 2 and 3: the Caveat note “She kept small things.” sits in `specimenNote` on a leader line to the violet and the latch, over a 12 px paper halo; it never covers the form. Shot 4: the same note sits in `quoteNote` on a leader from the cup. The title has no leader; the mist is silent; the close carries a closing line, which the style allows, not an annotation leader.
- **No vector outline:** Shot 1: the window arch's edge is a brush edge, not a uniform vector line. Shot 2: the violet's contour is a darker brush stroke that pools and breaks. Shot 3: the latch's edge is a variable-width ink stroke. Shot 6: the open gap proves the cup handle has no closed outline; the form is still being painted when the film ends.

## Questions

- **Names slot.** `recipes/watercolor_title.json` lists `content.names` and a `names` text op; `APPLICATION.md` says `names` is read by no recipe. Which is the contract? I filled “Nora Vale”; if the resolver ignores it, the title's names line is lost and the 9:16 `names` box is unused.
- **9:16 box mapping.** The recipes carry 1:1 coordinates; the 9:16 boxes are a recomposition, but no recipe maps an op to a box. Which box does the specimen's `place` label use—`label` (y 0.82) or `labelVenue` (y 0.76)? The 9:16 `specimenNote` (y 0.3) and `quoteNote` (y 0.6) also sit above the style's “caption sits in the bottom safe zone.” Does the resolver remap the recipe ops to the 9:16 boxes, or must the lead remap by hand? I state my mapping above. The title's `arch` (x 0.58–0.92, y 0.22–0.62) and `monogram` (x 0.84–0.96, y 0.20–0.25) boxes overlap; I place the monogram on the upper right jamb, not in the window opening. Is that the intended reading?
- **Paper gap.** `watercolor_close.json` fixes `paper-gap` at x 0.62, y 0.18, w 0.06, h 0.34. I composed the cup handle's join to that rectangle; may the gap be re-anchored to the painted form's edge instead, as the style's “last wash pass stops before the form's edge” suggests? I kept the fixed rectangle and composed the form to it.
- **Paint-in scope.** Does the single 1.2 s / 1.8 s choice apply to every painted form, including the title's window arch and the close's unfinished cup handle, or only to specimens and quote objects? I chose 1.8 s for every painted form; the mist is a wipe and the seal is a stamp.
- **Accent count.** Does the same `prop.stamp-seal` at the title and the close count as one vermilion mark recurring, or two marks? I read it as one mark, twice; the mist carries none.
- **Mist and wipe.** The quote's `transitionOut` is `wipe`; the mist is also a wipe and composites over the outgoing shot. Does the quote's wipe hand off to the mist, or does the quote need to hold so the mist has something to erase? I assumed the quote's wipe is the handoff and the mist is the erase.
- **Song/voice.** The pack says voice is optional; the commission forbids third-party voice and lyrics. Is a wordless solo felt-piano motif enough for a “song/memory film,” or does the commission expect a sung song? I planned no voice and no lyric.
- **Whole milliseconds (minor).** The share rule gives 666.667 / 833.333 / 833.333 / 666.667 / 500 / 500 ms. To whole milliseconds that is 667 / 833 / 833 / 667 / 500 / 500, which sums to 30.000 s. Is the resolver's rounding nearest, floor-plus-remainder, or largest-remainder? No .5 tie occurs in this route, so the six seconds are stable.
