# Questions — A Small Inventory

The pack fixes the six recipes, the page contracts, the palette roles, the type mapping, the transition set and the 30 s and 9:16 limits. These are the questions it leaves open for this film. The first is the pack's one declared choice; the rest are application questions.

## 1. paint-in-speed — the director's choice

**Question:** 1.2 s or 1.8 s per painted form?

**Fixed by the pack:** A specimen is the reference. The title's arch, the quote's object and the close's unfinished detail paint in by the same rule. The card shows both speeds side by side.

**What changes:** At 1.2 s each finished form settles earlier and sways longer within its shot; at 1.8 s the brush is still working deeper into the shot. The close's unfinished cup is the exception: it is left with its paper gap either way. The route, the six shots and the 30 s fit are unchanged.

**Status:** Open. The director chooses at review. This treatment does not answer it.

## 2. The mist on the 9:16 page

The mist recipe has no `vertical` array. Its `mist` and `mist-full` shapes already use full-frame normalised coordinates. Does the 9:16 page use them as authored, or does the mist need a 9:16 box mapping like the other four page contracts? This treatment reads the full-frame shapes as aspect-independent. If that is wrong, the wavy edge must still cross the full 390 px width and end on full paper.

## 3. What `names` means in a memory film

The pack reads `content.names`, but its BRIEF describes names as the wedding couple. For a memory film, does `names` mean the remembered person, the rememberer, or both? This treatment reads it as both — "Ines & Mara", the remembered and the rememberer — and uses the monogram "IM". If the pack intends only the remembered person, the brief should read "Ines" and the monogram "I".

## 4. One caption, four recurrences

The pack gives one value per slot. This film uses the same caption in four places: the two specimen notes, the quote note and the close's closing line. The pack allows the close to share the caption as a refrain and says a repeated caption is a running note, but it does not say whether four recurrences is the intended limit. Is the refrain the director's intent, or should S2 carry per-shot notes? The same question applies to the venue label, which appears in the title and on both specimens.

## 5. The resolver seed

The pack seeds stroke phase, paper fibre offset, wash-edge jitter, paint-in order and sway phase, but the brief and format schemas have no seed field. Which seed should the resolver use? The treatment assumes one seed for the whole film, so the two specimen pages and the close's cup share a consistent hand.
