The promise

A quiet page that holds one moment.

Warm paper, dark ink, one muted accent and enough time to read the caption. The style is a book page, not a poster.

What it is

A reusable system for short, page-led films built from photographs, paper and captions.

What it refuses

Gloss, gold, sparkle, fast cuts and any frame that needs a fifth colour.

What it needs

One hero photograph, a short caption and a real date. No on-camera cast.

Motion

Signature move: one slow page turn, then a held page. The spread is held for 5.6 s, the turn takes 1.6 s, and the loop is 7.2 s. No bounce, no overshoot, no camera shake.

Held spread5.6 s
Page turn1.6 s
Card loop7.2 s

The page turns from the left edge at reading speed. If the caption cannot be read before the turn begins, the shot is too short. At the loop boundary the card returns to the first page; the film only turns forward.

Sound

Room tone only, or silence. No music unless the brief asks for it.

Rules

Five observable dos

  1. Put every element on a paper surface.
  2. Make the name or the caption the largest thing in the frame.
  3. Keep at least seven percent margin on every side.
  4. Finish each move before the next one starts.
  5. Leave one quiet area in every spread.

Three refusal examples

  • No gloss, foil or metallic sheen.
  • No more than one accent colour in a frame.
  • No stock-photo smiles pasted into a page.
