The promise

A quiet page that holds one ordinary thing.

Warm off-white paper, dark ink and one muted terracotta mark. A still photograph, a dense caption, and enough time to read it. This is a family archive kept in a cupboard, not a film made to impress a room.

Feels

warm · patient · unsentimental

Never feels

glossy · ceremonial · like a wedding invitation or a slideshow

SPECIMEN All names, dates and captions on this card are specimen copy.

What it is

A reusable system for short, page-led family archive films built from still photographs, warm paper and dense captions.

What it refuses

Gloss, gold, sparkle, fast cuts, music and any frame that needs a fifth colour.

What it needs

One hero photograph, one supporting detail at most, an 18-word caption and a real date. No on-camera cast.

Palette

Default palette: sunday-paper. Six roles exist; no frame may use more than four. A photograph counts as one colour. The neutral role is slate, a matte stone; there is no metal role and no metallic sheen.

Motion

Signature move: one slow page turn, then a held page. The spread is held for 5.6 s, the turn takes 1.6 s, and the loop is 7.2 s. No bounce, no overshoot, no camera shake.

Held spread5.6 s
Page turn1.6 s
Card loop7.2 s

The page turns from the left edge at reading speed. If the caption cannot be read before the turn begins, the shot is too short. At the loop boundary the card returns to the first page; the film only turns forward.

The director's choice: the page-turn speed is recorded in capabilities.governance.choices as page-turn-speed. The phone shows 1.6 s; the comparison below shows 1.2 s at the same 5.6 s hold.

Sound

Room tone only, or silence. A clock, a kettle, a chair, a garden. No music, no voice-over.

Silence is preferred on the first and last spread.

Rules

Five observable dos

  1. Put every element on one sheet of warm paper.
  2. Hold the page until the caption is read, then turn.
  3. Keep at least seven percent margin on every side.
  4. Let the caption be the densest thing in the frame.
  5. Leave one quiet area in every spread.

Three refusal examples

  • No gloss, foil, gold or metallic sheen.
  • No more than four colours in a frame; a photograph counts as one.
  • No stock-photo smiles, confetti or wedding-stationery motifs.
