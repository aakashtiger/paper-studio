# Segment C — 38.50–58.94

## Method

- **Source:** `out/claude-pop.mp4`, 1920x1080, 30 fps. Segment C = the five shots `gym`, `singularity`, `accel`, `atoms`, `cage` (manifest #14–18). Picture frames 1155–1768; 614 frames; 20.47 s of picture.
- **Primary review:** `out/analysis/shots-15/act2-gym.png` … `act2-cage.png` — one cell per 15 fps sample (every 2nd 30 fps frame), 8 columns. **Cut review:** `out/analysis/cuts-30/c14-gym.png` … `c18-cage.png` — 30 consecutive 30 fps frames around each cut.
- **Motion:** `out/analysis/frame-diff.json` — mean absolute luma difference at 30 fps on a 192x108 reduction, 3x3 region map, per-shot stats. The row index `i` is the first frame of the new state and its `t` is `(i+1)/30`, one frame late; I use `i/30`. Actual picture cuts: **1155 (38.500), 1241 (41.367), 1350 (45.000), 1486 (49.533), 1584 (52.800), 1769 (58.967)**. The authored 44.98 cut lands on frame 1350; 41.36 on 1241; 49.52 on 1486. The 15 fps sheets sample even frames, so they miss the odd cuts at 1155, 1241 and 1769 — the 30 fps strips are authoritative.
- **Visual confirmation:** native 1920x1080 frames at 39.0, 40.6, 43.0, 45.0, 45.2, 46.0, 47.0, 48.2, 50.4, 51.0, 52.4, 53.8, 55.0, 58.4, 58.9, plus 2x crops of the gym left side and SOL at 39.0/40.6 (`out/analysis/tmp/gym_left.png`, `zoom_sol_gym.png`, `zoom_sol_gym2.png`). Every soft run below was checked in a native frame or a crop.
- **Code:** `src/scenes/act2.js` (gym 381–411, singularity 413–464, accel 466–547, atoms 549–594, cage 596–696), `src/core.js`, `src/type.js`, `src/characters.js`, `src/props.js` (`gymSet` 284–332, `drawTV` 359–377), `src/scene_kit.js`, `src/timeline.js`, `src/lyrics.js` (l11–l15, 19–23).
- **Caveats:** Grain (24 Hz), paper-texture offset (2 Hz), type boil (12 Hz), the frame counter and the P(DOOM) ticker change in every shot; **they are not counted as motion**. `frame-diff` underweights thin type and cream-on-cream edges at 192x108; the quiet runs below are confirmed visually. The picture frame counts are 86 / 109 / 136 / 98 / 185; the manifest's 87 / 109 / 138 / 99 / 185 are inclusive or rounded, so the manifest is one high for gym and atoms and two high for accel. The 15 fps sheets and the code agree on content, but **not** on the accel `FASTER` fade (see accel flaws) and not on the storyboard's camera language.

**Verdict.** This segment is a **motion poster**: the type is the star, the set is the world, the cast is a paper doll. The strongest shot is **singularity** (41.36–44.98): mean diff 5.663, median 4.031, nine moving layers, the only shot where type, set and cast move together. The weakest is **cage** (52.80–58.94): 136 consecutive frames (4.50 s, 73% of the shot) below 2.0, a 5 px character bounce, a 22 px bar bow and a cage that does not read as a cage. Second weakest is **gym**: 2.86 s of a 2-layer stage, a double LOSS card, a treadmill that does not move, and a rig that comes apart. The graphic design is strong; the motion, rig, contact and type roles are not.
