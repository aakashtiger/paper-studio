# CLAUDE POP — shot list

Legend: **BG** background · **FG** foreground · **TXT** typography · **→** transition out.
Every shot is a pure function of song time. Camera is 2.5D: `bg / mid / character / fg / type`.

## Recurring visual gags
- The DOOM meter is always one beat behind the lyric.
- A paperclip appears in every act (foreshadowing).
- The number 34 / 61 / 86 / 99.9 appears as a folio number in the corner.
- MIRA's glasses reflect whatever is about to happen.
- One Petal always waves at the wrong time.
- The moon is on a visible string in wide shots.
- A tiny "© 2026 PAPER SINGULARITY" bug at the bottom.
