# The Understudy — script

**Format:** 30.0 s · 16:9 · 30 fps · narration + SFX, no music
**Style:** Paper K-pop 0.1.0
**Cast:** Nova Kim (the star), Mira (the understudy)
**One line:** The cue lamp calls Nova; the wings are empty; Mira walks on; the
crowd never knows.

## Narration

1. Every night, the cue lamp calls one name.
2. Tonight, it called Nova.
3. But the wings were empty.
4. The understudy knew the light.
5. Mira walked on.
6. The crowd never knew.

## Direction notes

- One signature: the sticker slam. One transition: the iris.
- Palette: paper, ink, red, blue; gold only on the title and the sticker.
- The stage is a paper diorama: hard contact shadows, one top-left key, torn
  backdrops, die-cut cast.
- The duet is eight counts, planted feet, head counter-rotation, three
  half-beats apart.
- No music. The narration is the clock; SFX mark the hits.
